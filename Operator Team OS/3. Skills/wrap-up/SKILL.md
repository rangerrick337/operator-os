# Wrap-Up

Run two questions in order:

1. **Confidence audit:** What remains unverified or assumption-dependent? For each
   material item, state why confidence is limited and the exact evidence or test
   that would resolve it.
2. **Blind-spot review:** What is the single biggest thing the user may be missing?
   Then name up to three second-order effects or hidden dependencies.

For product or business work, optionally add one unrequested idea only when it is
specific, differentiated, and worth its cost or complexity. Name the tradeoff.

Distinguish known facts from hypotheses. Do not manufacture uncertainty, auto-run
new work, or treat reflection as approval. Wait for the user to choose which gap,
if any, to investigate.
